| A-027 | Phase 2 catalogue and stock model:<br>- `KiranaMaterial` stays the product table.<br>- Quantities are Decimal(14,3) in a fixed `baseUnit`, with per-product unit factors (metric fixed, pack sizes whole over a count base). Conversions never round.<br>- Stock changes only through `applyMovement`, which updates `StockBalance` and appends `StockMovement` in one transaction (balance-row lock, `balanceAfter`).<br>- Only SALE may take stock negative.<br>- Price, cost and tax changes append `ProductPriceChange` snapshots.<br>- Purchase cost is hidden without `catalog.cost.view`.<br>- Barcodes are unique per business, including archived products. | Built 2026-11-16 (`ff72475`). Rejected: storing stock on the product row, and float quantities. |
| A-028 | Phase 3 POS model:<br>- **Held bills:** a held bill is a server-side draft (`HeldBill`, with a JSON `lines` snapshot validated by zod). It never writes stock, sales, payments or ledgers. Any `pos.use` member of the same store can list and recall it.<br>- **Hold and recall** (amended 2026-11-18 after the review): a hold takes an optional `idempotencyKey`, so a retry returns the first bill. Recall *claims* the bill with the till's `claimKey` in one conditional update. The same key may retry, another till gets 404, and claims lapse after 2 min. The till then confirms with `DELETE /pos/held/:id?claimKey=`. That marks the bill recalled (`recalledAt`; kept, hidden from list and recall) instead of deleting it, so a confirm under any other claim gets 409, even one arriving after the other till has confirmed (live-pass D1). A lost response or a closed window no longer loses the bill.<br>- **Pricing** (amended 2026-11-18): the line gross is `round2_half_up(qty × factor(unit) × sellingPrice / factor(salesUnit))`, never built from a rounded unit price. Otherwise 750 g at ₹45/kg would bill ₹37.50, and picking a smaller unit would be a hidden discount. The 2dp `unitPrice` is for display, and sending it back is not an override. A different client price needs `pos.price.override` (gross = round2(qty × unitPrice)). A non-zero discount needs `pos.discount` and is capped at the gross. Both permissions go to OWNER and MANAGER, not CASHIER.<br>- **Cart preview:** kirana-F keeps money in integer paise and quantities in integer thousandths. The server stays the authority.<br>- **Variants:** pack units (CARTON/PIECE…) are the POS "variants"; there are no variant groups (A-027).<br>- **Tax:** no GST in the preview; that's Phase 4 invoices. | Drafts are not financial records, so they may be dropped once their till confirms; they are kept, marked, only so a late confirm can be refused. JSON lines avoid a second table for data that is only ever loaded whole. Snapshotting on the server means a tampered client can't change a price. |
| A-029 | Desktop wraps kirana-F (owner, 2026-11-17). `Kirana-desktop` is only the Tauri shell: Rust window, guards, CSP and the token-owning commands. Its UI is kirana-F's build. Inside Tauri, kirana-F's `src/api/client.ts` sends calls over IPC (`api_request`, `login`, `logout`, `restore_session`) instead of fetch + cookies. One POS codebase serves web and desktop, and Phase 9 offline work lands in kirana-F too. Cost: a desktop build depends on the checked-out `../kirana-F`, and kirana-F must stay within the desktop CSP. |
